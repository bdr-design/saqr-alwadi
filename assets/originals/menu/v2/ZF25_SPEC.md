ZF-25 — ENGINEERING SPECIFICATION (fictional aircraft; all figures in metres, stations measured aft from the nose tip)

- Belly: flat-bottomed and smooth between the intakes and the tail.

DORSAL HUMP: behind the canopy a rounded spine from station 7.6 to 16.5, standing 0.3 above the fuselage top line, sloping down into the space between the two fins.

AIR INTAKES (the aircraft's most distinctive feature): TWO separate ROUND intakes UNDER THE BELLY, side by side, their faces at station 6.0 (below the rear seat), each 0.9 in diameter with a thick round lip, their centres 0.55 either side of the centreline, a 0.2 gap between them, hanging below the belly line. Behind them each duct is faired smoothly into the belly up to station 10.5. NOTHING on the fuselage sides: no side intakes, no leading-edge root extensions, no strakes.

WING: a CROPPED DELTA at mid-fuselage height, flat (no dihedral, no anhedral).
- Root: leading edge at station 7.8, trailing edge at station 15.0 (root chord 7.2).
- Tip: at 6.2 from the centreline, tip chord 1.6, the tip edge parallel to the fuselage, its corners slightly rounded.
- Leading edge: ONE straight line swept 49 degrees.
- Trailing edge: ONE STRAIGHT LINE exactly at right angles to the fuselage, with flaps and ailerons along it.
- No wingtip missile rails. Three pylons under each wing at 2.3, 3.8 and 5.0 from the centreline, plus two pylons under the belly behind the intakes.

FINS: two vertical fins standing on top of the engine nacelles, 1.0 either side of the centreline, canted OUTWARD only 8 degrees; root from station 14.8 to 17.6; height 2.55 above the fuselage; leading edge swept 40 degrees; tip chord 0.9 with a rounded tip; the trailing edge almost vertical.

PAINT: upper surfaces medium-dark grey, lower surfaces light grey, the line between them along the fuselage sides at wing level; slightly darker radome; ordinary panel lines and rivets. No national insignia, no roundels, no flags, no numbers, no text, no logos.

WHAT IT MUST NOT LOOK LIKE: an F/A-18 (no leading-edge extensions, no side intakes), an F-15 (no box intakes, no straight-up fins), an F-16 (no chin intake, single fin), an F-22, F-35, J-20 or Su-57 (no stealth facets, no diamond wing), a Rafale, Typhoon or Gripen (no canards), a Su-27/MiG-29 (no separated nacelles with a tail boom between the nozzles).
