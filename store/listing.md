• 37 مهمة وحملة متتابعة: إسناد قوات تحت النار، ومرافقة رتل، واعتراض بحري، وأهداف حساسة للوقت، وعمليات بعدة مراحل.

• لاسلكي بأصوات مسجّلة بنبرة الشبكات العسكرية.
• محرر مهام تصنع فيه مهمتك بنفسك.
• اللعبة كلها بالعربية أو بالإنجليزية.

محاكاة خيالية: الأماكن والأحداث من الخيال، ولا علاقة لها بأي جهة رسمية.

## English (en-US)

**App name** (30 characters at most):

```
Saqr Al-Wadi: Air Combat Sim
```

**Short description** (80 characters at most):

```
Sit in the cockpit of a strike jet and an attack helicopter. Realistic sim.
```

**Full description:**

```
Saqr Al-Wadi is a realistic combat flight simulator that puts you in the weapons seat: the pilot flies, and you work the sensors and the weapons with the aircraft's own controls.

• Two aircraft, two seats: the gunner in the front seat of an attack helicopter, and the weapon systems officer in the back seat of a strike jet.
• A targeting pod as on the real thing: track and zoom, day TV, night TV and thermal, and a laser that guides the bomb all the way in.
• A photo-real cockpit: the multi-function displays, their pages, the grips and the master arm.
• Four maps in aerial imagery: the valley, the desert, the port and the camp, and the whole theatre with its base and its coast.
• 37 missions and a campaign: troops in contact, convoy escort, maritime interdiction, time-sensitive targets, operations in several phases.
• Rules of engagement: identify before you release, watch for civilians, and respect danger close to your own troops.
• Real threats: anti-aircraft guns, shoulder-launched missiles and a radar air defence to defeat with flares, chaff and a hard break.
• Day, dusk and night; clear skies and dust.
• Radio calls recorded in the voice of a military net.
• A mission editor to build your own.
• The whole game in English or Arabic.
• Plays offline, with no ads, and collects no data.

A work of fiction: its places and events are imagined, and it has no connection with any official body.
```
